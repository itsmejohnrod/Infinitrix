INFINITRIX SOUND AND LAYOUT UPDATE

Stop the server with Ctrl+C. Back up your project. Merge the contents of the
ZIP's Infinitrix folder into your existing project; replace matching files.
Keep your .env, .venv and instance folder. Do not delete the old project.
Restart the server and hard-refresh the browser (Ctrl+Shift+R).

Sound: use the new Sound button in the top bar, or Settings > Audio.
Settings volume controls the five API games and interface click sounds.
Sounds are synthesized locally; no extra files or sound services are needed.
The two arcade games retain their own sound toggles. Inputs, answers and
memory moves get feedback tones. API games do not play background music.
Gentle page and card animations are enabled when reduced motion is off.
Settings > Display > Reduced motion can disable optional animations; your
browser's reduced-motion preference also takes precedence. No flashing added.

Layout: logo fits the sidebar, canvases preserve their original aspect ratio,
platformer touch controls sit below the game and cannot be selected as text.
